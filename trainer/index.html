Beat the clock

You'll get the name of a city, landmark or natural feature. Find it on the globe and drop a pin as fast as you can.

  • 15 seconds for the first place.
  • Each place scores up to 100. Your next clock is 15 seconds × that score ÷ 100, so a 90 gives you 13.5 seconds.
  • Below 70 ends the run (that's about 1,000 miles off). So does running out of time.

Just for practice. Nothing is saved.
